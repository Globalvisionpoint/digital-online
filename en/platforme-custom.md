# Custom Platforms & Applications | Digital Online

> We create platforms and applications from scratch, exactly the way you need them. Tell us the idea, we build it.

## What kinds of platforms we build

From MVPs to enterprise platforms, using modern, scalable technologies.

#### Mobile apps (iOS + Android)

Native or cross-platform apps with React Native, Flutter, Swift and Kotlin. Published to the App Store and Google Play.

#### Web platforms (SaaS)

Complex web apps with dashboards, authentication, payments and an admin panel. Stack: Next.js, React, Node.js, Python.

#### Marketplaces

Multi-vendor platforms with escrow, split payments, seller dashboards, products and orders.

#### Internal dashboards

Admin panels with data views, KPIs, reports and alerts, for management and teams.

#### Booking systems

Online booking platforms for clinics, salons, freelancers, sports venues and more.

#### E-learning platforms

LMS platforms with video courses, quizzes, certificates, payments, progress tracking and gamification.

#### Internal social networks

Private communities, forums, chat, feeds, profiles and groups for companies or niches.

#### Custom CRMs

Customer management systems, sales pipeline, automation and reporting.

#### APIs & integrations

REST/GraphQL API development, microservices and integrations with third-party systems.

#### Platforms with built-in AI

Applications with AI capabilities: chat, recommendations, image analysis, content generation.

#### Loyalty schemes

Loyalty schemes: points, rewards, referral and gamification for customers.

#### Management systems

Custom ERP, stock management, logistics and warehouse management.

## How a custom project runs

Four clear stages, with a deliverable at each step and full visibility throughout.

#### Discussion & vision

Tell us your idea. We discuss the goals, the target audience and the problems the application solves.

#### Specs & design

We write technical and functional specifications. We create wireframes and a complete UI/UX design.

#### Iterative development

We build in sprints, with regular demos. You refine the product as we go.

#### Testing & launch

We test rigorously, secure and launch to production. We provide support after launch.
