PRIVACY

Data protection, GDPR
EXPLORE

1. What data we collect

The data processed depends on how you choose to contact us:

  • Requests by email, phone or WhatsApp: the data you send us, such as your name, contact details and the content of your request. The provider of the chosen channel processes that data under its own policies.
  • The form on the main page: in the current version the form is a demo. The data entered is not sent to a server and never reaches Digital Online; after you try to submit, the form shows an informative message and keeps the data only in the open page.
  • Technical data: the hosting provider receives connection data such as IP address, browser type and the requested resource, needed to deliver the pages and keep the site secure. Details in section 5.
  • Cookie preferences: your choice for the necessary, analytics and marketing categories, saved in your browser local storage. Details in Cookie policy.
  • In the site code reviewed on 7 October 2024, no Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel or other analytics or advertising scripts are installed.

2. Legal basis for processing

Depending on the request and the stage of the relationship, the legal basis may be:

  • Steps at your request before a contract is signed (art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), to answer a request for a quote.
  • Legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), for the operation and security of the website, within the applicable limits.
  • Legal obligation (art. 6(1)(c) GDPR), where a contractual relationship or a relevant legal obligation arises.
  • Consent (art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), if optional technologies requiring it are introduced. At present the site activates no analytics or advertising tools.

3. Purpose of processing

The data received through the contact channels is used for:

  • Replying to the request sent by email, phone or WhatsApp and, where relevant, preparing a quote.
  • Managing the contractual relationship and meeting legal obligations, if a contract is signed.
  • Ensuring the site runs and stays secure.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

Details about the local storage of your choice, the banner options, how long the preference lasts and the tools in use are described in Cookie policy. You can reopen the settings from the site footer or from this link.

5. Sharing data with third parties

We do not sell your data. Depending on the channel used and the infrastructure in place, the data may be processed by:

  • Hosting provider: Vercel Inc., which delivers the site through its global distribution network. Hosting is configured in the European Union region (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Provider of the chosen channel, for example the email service or WhatsApp, if you contact us through it.
  • Public authorities, only where there is a legal obligation.

We use no font, analytics or advertising providers. The site loads no third-party resources.

6. International transfers

The hosting provider, Vercel Inc., is a company based in the United States, and the infrastructure used for this site is configured in the Frankfurt region, Germany. Any transfer outside the European Economic Area relies on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. The site loads no analytics or advertising tools.

7. How long we keep your data

  • The demo form: the data entered is not transmitted to or stored on a server in the current version.
  • Cookie preference: 180 days in your browser local storage.
  • Messages received by email or WhatsApp: up to 24 months from the last contact, after which they are deleted, except for documents that must be kept for legal obligations.
  • Hosting technical logs: up to 30 days, the hosting provider standard retention period.

8. Your rights under the GDPR

You have the following rights, which you can exercise at any time and free of charge by writing to us at contact@digital-online.ro:

  • Right of access (art. 15): to find out what data we process about you.
  • Right to rectification (art. 16): to correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure (the "right to be forgotten", art. 17).
  • Right to restrict processing (art. 18).
  • Right to data portability (art. 20).
  • Right to object (art. 21), including with regard to direct marketing.
  • Right not to be subject to automated decision-making (art. 22).
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time (art. 7(3)).
  • Right to lodge a complaint with the ANSPDCP (www.dataprotection.ro) or with a supervisory authority in the EU.

We respond to your requests within 30 days from receipt.

9. Data security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risks, in line with art. 32 GDPR: encrypted connections over HTTPS, restrictive security headers, restricted access to contact data and no third-party scripts that could expose visitor data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any change will be published on this page, with the date of the update. We encourage you to review the policy periodically.
