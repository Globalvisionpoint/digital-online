# Automated Integrations | Digital Online

> Automated integrations with invoicing, courier and payment systems. Everything one click apart.

## What we integrate for you

All the systems a working online store needs, connected to each other.

#### SmartBill

Automatic invoicing with every order. Customers, products and payments synchronised. VAT calculated correctly, e-Factura respected.

#### Oblio / Fgo

Integration with alternative invoicing platforms. We issue proforma and fiscal invoices and corrections automatically.

#### Fan Courier

Shipping labels generated straight from the order. Live status tracking and printable labels.

#### Sameday

Full integration with Sameday: label generation, easybox lockers, home delivery, automatic tracking.

#### Cargus

Connection with Cargus: automatic labels, synchronised status, dynamically calculated costs.

#### GLS / DHL / DPD

Integration with any international or national courier, on request.

#### Stripe

Card payment processing: Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay. 3D Secure included.

#### Netopia / MobilPay

A Romanian processor supporting all local banks, interest-free instalments and instalment payments.

#### PayU Romania

Online payments with full support: card, bank transfer, instalments, cash on delivery.

#### Cash on delivery

Order management with cash on delivery, automated courier workflows.

#### Email marketing

Integration with Mailchimp and Klaviyo: automated post-order emails, abandoned cart, customer win-back.

#### Shopify / WooCommerce

Connection with any e-commerce platform: Shopify, WooCommerce, custom.
