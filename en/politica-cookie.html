Cookie policy

Last updated 7 October 2024. This page describes the current version of the site code and must be reviewed whenever new services or scripts are added.

What the site uses right now

The preference manager stores your choice in your browser local storage, under the key digital-online-cookie-consent, for up to 180 days. It remembers whether you accepted or rejected the optional categories. The manager sets no HTTP cookies.

As of this update, the site code includes no analytics, advertising or remarketing tools such as Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or TikTok Pixel. The analytics and marketing categories in the settings do not activate services that are not installed.

Categories and options

  • Strictly necessary: local storage of your choice, so we do not show you the banner on every page. These are always active.
  • Analytics: is not used in the current version of the site.
  • Marketing: is not used in the current version of the site.

You can choose "Reject optional", "Accept all" or "Settings". Declining is as easy as accepting. You can reopen your preferences from the "Cookie settings" link in the footer of every page.

Your preference expires after 180 days. If you clear your browser storage or use a different device or browser, you will be asked to choose again.

External resources

The site does not load fonts, scripts or other third-party resources. The fonts used are hosted on the same domain, and the pages make no requests to Google Fonts, CDNFonts or other external libraries. The only provider involved in delivering the pages is the hosting provider, described in Privacy policy.

Changing your choice

Open "Cookie settings" in the footer, change the options and save. You can also delete the record in your browser settings. If the technologies used by the site change, we update this policy and the consent version.
