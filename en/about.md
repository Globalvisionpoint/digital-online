# About Digital Online | Who we are and how we work

> Who Digital Online is: the team behind Global Vision Point Limited, how we work, what we deliver and where to find our legal details.

ABOUT US

Digital Online is the brand under which Global Vision Point Limited builds and maintains the online presence of small and medium businesses: presentation sites, online stores, custom platforms, automated integrations, AI implementations and the campaigns that bring traffic to them. We work as one team, with design, code, SEO and promotion in the same place, precisely so you do not get a beautiful site that nobody finds.

## Who we are

Global Vision Point Limited is the company behind the brand, with its registered office at Suite 2, 260 Main Street, Gibraltar, GX11 1AA. The team that actually works on projects covers Romania and the European Union, and we communicate in Romanian, English, Spanish and Portuguese.

We are not a freelancer network and we do not resell templates. Every project is built for the company that commissions it, starting from its services, prices and the way it actually works.

## What we deliver

- Websites & IT Services  — presentation sites and landing pages, fast and optimised for search.
- Online Stores  — Shopify and custom store implementations, with payments, delivery and invoicing connected.
- Automated integrations  — links between store, accounting, courier and CRM, with no manual data entry.
- AI implementation & chatbots  — assistants that answer from your real data, not from invented replies.
- Custom Platforms  — internal applications and customer portals, built around your requirements.
- Ads & Promotion  — Google Ads, Meta Ads and technical SEO, with monthly reporting.

## How we work

We start with a short conversation about what you need and what you have today. A written quote follows, with scope, deadline and a fixed price, so there are no surprises at the end. We build in stages, with a preview link you can open at any time, and the launch happens only after you approve the final version. After launch we stay available for changes and maintenance.

You can find what each service includes on the [IT services](https://digital-online.ro/servicii-it.html), and the full steps are described in the section [our process](https://digital-online.ro/#proces).
