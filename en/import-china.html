Global logistics, simplified.

From the Chinese supplier to your warehouse: sourcing, freight, customs and compliance.

The import steps, simplified

Every step of the import explained clearly, with hands-on support at each stage.

Supplier sourcing

We find verified suppliers on Alibaba, 1688 and Made-in-China. We check legitimacy, quality and certifications.

Negotiation & samples

We negotiate price, MOQ, payment terms, packaging and labelling. We request samples and test quality.

Order & QC

We place the order, monitor production and run factory Quality Control before shipping.

Transport

We choose sea freight (LCL/FCL), air or courier (DHL, FedEx), optimising the balance of cost and time.

Customs clearance

We handle all customs formalities: HS codes, duties, VAT, declarations and EORI.

Domestic delivery

Transport from the port or airport to your warehouse. Optionally 3PL storage and fulfilment.

Tax obligations

Customs declarations, import VAT, Intrastat, accounting and correctly issued invoices.

Labelling & compliance

We ensure compliance with EU and Romanian rules: Romanian-language labels, CE marking, certifications.

Complete import consulting services

Supplier audit

We verify the supplier legal identity, licences, track record and genuine reviews.

International contracts

Bilingual contracts, Incoterms (EXW, FOB, CIF, DDP) and protective clauses.

Quality inspection

We send inspectors to the factory before shipping, with AQL checks and a detailed report with photos.

International payments

Bank transfers (T/T), L/C, PayPal, Western Union. Safe payment advice to avoid fraud.

Customs & duties

Tariff classification, duty and VAT calculation, excise duties and anti-dumping duties where they apply.

Packaging & labelling

We ensure packaging suited to the transport, Romanian labels, barcodes and EAN-13 codes.

Store listing

On delivery we help you list the products in your online store: titles, descriptions, images, SEO.

Cost optimisation

We analyse the total cost (product + freight + duties + storage) and optimise it for maximum margin.

Regulations & compliance

CE, RoHS, REACH, GPSR, mandatory certifications, customs restrictions and bans.
