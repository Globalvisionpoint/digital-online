# Privacy | Digital Online

> In short: what data digital-online.ro collects, why, how long we keep it and how to exercise your rights. The full text is in the privacy policy.

DATA PROTECTION

This page is the short, plain-language version of how we handle personal data. The complete, binding text is [Privacy policy](https://digital-online.ro/politica-confidentialitate.html); if the two ever differ, the complete policy applies.

## What data we collect

Only what you send us: your name, e-mail address, phone number and project description, when you write to us by e-mail, on WhatsApp or through the contact form. The site has no analytics tools, advertising pixels or remarketing, and loads no fonts or scripts from third parties, so it does not track your behaviour on other sites.

## Why we use them

To answer your message, prepare the quote and deliver the project. The basis is the discussion that precedes a contract and the performance of the contract, plus the legal invoicing and accounting obligations. We do not sell data and we do not use it for advertising sent without your consent.

## How long we keep them

Correspondence that does not lead to a project is kept for 24 months at most. Contract documents and invoices are kept for the period required by tax law. The cookie preference is kept in your browser for 180 days at most, as explained in [Cookie policy](https://digital-online.ro/privacy.html).

## Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to rectify it, to erase it, to restrict processing, to portability and to object. You can exercise them with a message to [contact@digital-online.ro](mailto:contact@digital-online.ro); we reply within one month at most. If that answer does not satisfy you, contact the supervisory authority in your country, or in Romania the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP).

## Who the controller is

The data controller is Global Vision Point Limited, with its registered office at Suite 2, 260 Main Street, Gibraltar, GX11 1AA, the company behind the Digital Online brand. The same details appear in [Terms and conditions](https://digital-online.ro/termeni-si-conditii.html), and the company presentation is on the [About Digital Online](https://digital-online.ro/about.html).
