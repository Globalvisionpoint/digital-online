## What to include in your first message

With a few details up front we can reply with a quote, not with questions:

- what you want to build or fix, in one sentence;
- a link to the current site, if there is one;
- the deadline you would like it finished by;
- a rough budget range, if you already have one;
- who makes the final decision in your company.

If you do not have all the answers yet, send the message anyway: together we work out what is possible within your budget and deadline.
